# 01x Cohort Plan - 100 days

# Mentor Personas Mapping

| Mentor | Persona | Core Strength | Phase Alignment | What They Help With |
|--------|--------|---------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Me | Builder + Systems Thinker | End-to-end execution, AI tooling, shipping systems | All Phases (1–4) | Maintaining momentum, connecting phases, enforcing execution, simplifying decisions |
| M1 | Product Thinker / 0→1 Founder | Problem validation, idea clarity, user understanding | Phase 1 — Clarity | Choosing the right problem, refining idea, defining MVP scope |
| M2 | Builder / Execution-focused Engineer | Fast shipping, MVP building, avoiding over-engineering | Phase 2 — Build | Building MVP fast, deciding what to skip, using tools effectively |
| M3 | Product + UX Thinker | User behavior, feedback loops, usability improvements | Phase 3 — Users | Interpreting feedback, improving UX, prioritizing fixes |
| M4 | Growth / Launch Operator | Distribution, positioning, early traction | Phase 4 — Launch | Crafting launch strategy, messaging, distribution channels |

### Dual Mentor Model

Each participant gets two mentors:

1. **Primary Mentor (full 14 weeks):** One of M1-M4 assigned as a dedicated mentor. Attends their Saturday reviews, knows their product deeply. Each mentor carries 2-3 participants as primaries. Weekday Slack access (async, not scheduled).

2. **Phase Specialist (rotating):** The existing model — relevant mentor leads the Sunday session for their phase. Brings domain expertise at the right moment.

# Cohort Composition

Target per cohort of 8-12 participants:

| Segment | % | Why |
|---------|---|-----|
| Technical builders (can code their MVP) | 40% | They ship. Without them, nothing gets built. |
| Product/design thinkers | 30% | Strong on problem framing + UX. Keep builders honest. |
| Business/growth operators | 20% | Distribution + positioning. Prevent "build it and they will come." |
| Domain experts | 10% | Deep vertical knowledge. Ground ideas in reality. |

**Peer review pairing:** Each participant is paired with someone from a different segment. They review each other's weekly deliverable before Saturday office hours. Technical person reviews the product person's positioning. Product person reviews the technical person's UX. Cross-pollination by design.

# Weekly Rhythm (constant across all weeks)

| Day | Purpose | Activity |
|-----|---------|----------|
| Sunday | Direction | Framework session + set weekly goal + assign deliverable |
| Mon-Tue | Build | Execution on weekly goal |
| Wednesday | Check-in | Async status update via Slack/form (3 questions) |
| Thu-Fri | Build | Continue execution + complete deliverable |
| Saturday | Review | Office hours + deliverable grading + peer review |

### Wednesday Async Check-in (3 Questions)

Not a meeting. A forcing function. Submitted via Slack or form by end of Wednesday:

1. What did you ship since Sunday?
2. What's blocking you?
3. What's your plan for Thu-Fri?

Primary mentor reads and responds async (~5 min per participant). Catches blockers 3 days earlier than Saturday.

---

# Graded Deliverable System

Every Sunday session produces a **mandatory deliverable** due by Friday. Graded on 3 levels:

| Level | Meaning | Criteria |
|-------|---------|----------|
| L1 (Ship-grade) | Ready to use / present | Complete, clear, demonstrates real understanding and effort |
| L2 (Draft-grade) | Good attempt, gaps remain | Mostly there, needs refinement — shows engagement |
| L3 (Incomplete) | Didn't engage meaningfully | Missing key components or low effort |

Resubmission is allowed once per deliverable to improve grade. Grading done by the phase specialist mentor on Saturday.

---

# Phase Gates

Progression between phases is **milestone-gated with time caps**, not purely calendar-driven.

| Gate | Between | Requirements to Pass |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| Gate 1 | Clarity → Build | Validated problem statement + 3 user conversations + at least 1 L1 deliverable from Weeks 1-2 |
| Gate 2 | Build → Users | Working MVP that solves the core problem + 10 users from guerrilla testing (Week 6) + at least 3 L1s and 2 L2s from Weeks 3-8 |
| Gate 3 | Users → Launch | At least 20 active users + documented feedback loop + retention signal |

**If someone doesn't pass a gate:** They get 1 extra week in that phase. If still not ready, they shift to "observer mode" — attend sessions, learn from peers, but don't pretend-launch a broken product. No shame in it. Better than shipping garbage.

## Final Insights

### Cohort is designed as a **gated execution system** — not a content-heavy program, not a calendar-driven conveyor belt.

* **Mentor's role is not to "teach a topic" but to:**
    - Guide decisions for that specific week
    - Grade deliverables with real feedback
    - Inject perspective from real experience
    - Help participants avoid common mistakes early
    - (Primary mentors) Maintain deep context on their assigned participants across all 14 weeks

* **Every week mentor does:**
    - **Sunday:** Lead the session (direction, key concepts, examples) + assign deliverable
    - **Wednesday:** Read async check-ins from primary mentees (~5 min each)
    - **Saturday:** Run office hours (review progress, grade deliverables, peer review, unblock participants)

* **The grading system creates real stakes:**
    - You can't coast. Every week has a deliverable.
    - You can't skip ahead. Phase gates block progression without evidence.
    - You can resubmit. The system rewards effort, not perfection.
    - Observer mode exists. No shame in learning without launching if the product isn't ready.

### Goal of the System

The goal is simple:

> Move participants from idea → validated problem → working product → 20 real users → public launch

If this system works, participants won't just learn — they will **consistently make progress and actually ship something real**. The ones who aren't ready won't ship broken products into the world — they'll know exactly where they stalled and what to fix next.
