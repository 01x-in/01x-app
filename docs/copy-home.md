## WHAT WE BELIEVE
> New section. Three belief statements in a 3-column layout.

**H2:** `What we believe.`

---

**Belief 1**
**Heading:** AI amplifies builders. It doesn't replace them.
**Body:** Building with AI well requires knowing what you're building and why. The best use of AI is to move faster on decisions you've already thought through — not to outsource the thinking itself.

---

**Belief 2**
**Heading:** Shipping is the only real education.
**Body:** You don't learn product thinking from a framework. You learn it by making hard decisions in the open, iterating on real feedback, and shipping things that can fail. That's how we learned.

---

**Belief 3**
**Heading:** Early adoption compounds.
**Body:** We were using AI to build products before it was mainstream. That experience — the dead ends, the breakthroughs, the changed workflows — is what we share. You don't have to learn it all the hard way.

## IS THIS FOR YOU?

**H2:** `Is 01x for you?`

**This is for you if:**
- ✓ You believe AI is a tool that makes good builders faster
- ✓ You want to build real products, not just study how to
- ✓ You're ready to ship something — not just explore an idea indefinitely
- ✓ You value a curated, high-signal environment
- ✓ You want to learn from people who were doing this before it was obvious

**This is not for you if:**
- × You're looking for passive content or pre-recorded courses
- × You need your idea fully validated before you commit to building it
- × You expect AI to handle the hard thinking
- × You're not comfortable building in the open, with peers
- × You need guarantees before you invest time or money

**Closing line:**
```
We'd rather you find that out now than on Day 50.
```
